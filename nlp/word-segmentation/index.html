How it works

Forward maximum matching reads from the left and always takes the longest word it knows, so in 结婚的和尚未结婚的 it takes 和尚 (monk) and misreads the rest. The graph above shows every word of the list that could start at each character; dynamic programming picks the path whose words are most likely together, the method of the jieba segmenter. The word list is small, with made-up frequencies, so the hand-written segmenters know only the words of the examples and a few more. Your browser's Intl.Segmenter uses the dictionary of its ICU library, which knows far more words but cannot be taught new ones.
